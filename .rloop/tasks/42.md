task.rs:261 uses `TaskId(u32::MAX)` as a magic sentinel for "all tasks" in resolve_all_dag. This is fragile and could collide with a real task.

## What to do

- Change `ExecutionPlan::target` from `TaskId` to `Option<TaskId>`
- `None` means "all tasks" (the --all case)
- `Some(id)` means a specific target task
- Update `resolve_all_dag` to set `target: None`
- Update `resolve_dag` to set `target: Some(id)`
- Update all consumers of `ExecutionPlan::target` in session.rs and main.rs
- Remove the `u32::MAX` sentinel comment
- Ensure all tests pass

## Why

Magic sentinel values are a known anti-pattern. Option<T> is the idiomatic Rust way to represent "maybe a value."
